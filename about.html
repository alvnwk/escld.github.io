The ESCLD aims to bring students and young people together around law, diplomacy, and European affairs.

Our mission

Giving student ideas and initiatives a platform.

The European Student Council for Law and Diplomacy is an independent student association dedicated to promoting the study, understanding, and dissemination of knowledge in the fields of law, diplomacy, international relations, and European affairs.

It aims to foster interdisciplinary dialogue, encourage academic exchange, and build connections between young people from diverse cultural and academic backgrounds.

Legal knowledge is most meaningful when combined with a broader understanding of institutions, societies, and the world around us.

Our principles

Values that guide our work.

I

Cooperation

Encouraging mutual support, knowledge-sharing, and collaborative projects among students and partners.

II

Pluralism

Embracing diverse experiences, perspectives, and legal traditions while fostering respectful debate.

III

Critical analysis

Encouraging critical analysis of international relations and judicial decisions involving diverse actors.,

Governance

A structured and participatory organisation.

The association operates in accordance with its statutes, internal regulations, and governing bodies. Thematic councils coordinate discussions and research within their respective fields, while working groups and specialised committees contribute to specific projects.

The association’s statutes and internal rules set out its structure, the ways members can get involved, and the responsibilities of each body.
