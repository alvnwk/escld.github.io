A decentralised organisation

The establishment of councils, departments, and committees.

The association is structured around several Councils, each overseeing different Departments. Each Department may include Committees dedicated to specific areas of work, which can be either permanent or independent.

The approach of the ESCLD
Our areas of interest

Interconnected disciplines.

A cross-disciplinary approach to contemporary legal, political, and international issues.

01

Law & Institutions

Explore legal systems, the theory of the state, constitutional law, and the functioning of institutions.

02

Diplomacy & International Relations

Understand relations between international actors, diplomatic negotiations, and the challenges of international cooperation.

03

Europe & Fundamental Rights

Study European governance, fundamental freedoms, and the protection of human rights.

Building our association

Learn together. Act with purpose.

The ESCLD is building a student-led structure organised around thematic councils and collaborative work. Its activities may include publications, debates, meetings, and academic cooperation projects.
